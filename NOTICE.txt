ALL LOGIC
=========

ALL LOGIC is an unofficial, independently modified host for logic analyzers.

It is based on DSView (DreamSourceLab), which is itself based on PulseView
from the sigrok project.

This software is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of:
  - DreamSourceLab / DSLogic / DSView
  - Sipeed / SLogic
  - PXLogic / PXView
  - WCH / Nanjing Qinheng
  - Alientek / ATK-Logic
  - Muse Lab / nanoDLA
  - or any other hardware vendor whose devices may appear in the device list

Device names such as "DSLogic" in the UI are the hardware product names
reported by the instrument. They do not mean official vendor support.

--------------------
Community drivers for non-DreamSourceLab analyzers exist only for
interoperability. Bundled firmware, if any, is provided solely to talk to
those devices. Do not present ALL LOGIC as an official vendor tool, and do
not ship another vendor's closed-source installer or private SDK with it.
